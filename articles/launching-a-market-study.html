Launching a market study: from an in-person event to a landing page

Two editions of Greener's Strategic Energy Storage Study, two launch formats: an event in São Paulo in 2025, and a landing page plus an online webinar in 2026.

Laura Magrin

In 2025 Greener released its first Strategic Energy Storage Study, as part of the company's positioning on innovation in the energy sector. I led the art direction and marketing of that launch, and in 2026 I built the landing page for the new edition.

2025: an event to open the conversation

The first edition was launched at an in-person event at Praça São Lourenço, in São Paulo, that debated the challenges and opportunities of energy storage in Brazil's energy transition.

I was responsible for the art direction, the multichannel promotion and the marketing strategy, plus the digital and print materials for the attendee experience, coordinating internal areas and external partners to keep the brand consistent.

The edition sold 40 copies, and its free sample was downloaded 1,500 times.

2026: the launch moves online

In November 2026 Greener released the new edition, published as the Strategic Energy Storage Study 2027, with the market's 2026 data. This time the launch was an online webinar, on 5 November, together with the study's landing page on greener.com.br.

I built and published that page. It brings together the study highlights, a side-by-side comparison of the free sample and the full edition, and the lead forms.

What the two launches have in common

  • A free sample opens the door. It lets the market get to know the study before deciding to buy, so the 2026 page shows the sample and the full edition side by side.
  • One visual language, from the event to the page. The signage at the 2025 launch and the 2026 landing page belong to the same study, and they should look like it.
  • The launch is also lead generation. Forms connected to the marketing platform turn interest in the study into contacts the team can follow up.

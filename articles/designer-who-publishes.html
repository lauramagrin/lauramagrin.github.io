A designer who publishes: shipping pages to a company website with AI-assisted code

I don't hand-write code. Since October 2026 I have been publishing pages to greener.com.br anyway, with Claude Code and a workflow where every change is previewed before it goes live.

Laura Magrin

Greener works across three pillars: Market Intelligence, Events and Education. The company website, launched in July 2025, brings them together: the latest study, open courses, the next immersion, the Summit and the Greener Insights library. Since launch it has passed 140K page views and 62K unique visitors, and in September 2026 it had 2.3 times as many visitors as in September 2025.

I manage the website project and its visual direction, and design layouts with the team. Since late October 2026, I also publish to the site myself.

Vibe coding, with guardrails

I don't hand-write code. I build with AI in Claude Code, alongside Greener's developers. Before shipping anything, I set up rules for the repository: every change goes through a branch, a pull request and an automatic preview before it reaches the live site. Nothing goes straight to production, and each change can be checked in a real browser before visitors see it.

What went live in the first weeks

  • New pages: the landing page for the Strategic Energy Storage Study 2027, a new Education page that brings together the online course and the in-person immersions, the English version of the Greener Immersion page, and the article “Energy storage advances in regulation” in Portuguese and English.
  • Fixes: new RD Station forms across the study pages, including a fix to the Send button.
  • Updates: the home page's featured studies now read automatically from the library page, plus a refreshed sponsor section and a reorganised menu.
  • Redirects: permanent redirects so old immersion and education links lead to the current pages.

Copy and structure still come first

Code is the last step, not the first. Larissa Maria works with me on the copy and on structuring each page, and the layouts come from the design team. What changed is the distance between an approved layout and a live page: for launches and fixes, that distance now runs through me.

To show what this looks like in practice, the greener.com.br case has interactive demos based on the pages we produced, with fictional content: a landing page, the home page, the Education page, a lead form and the redirect rules.

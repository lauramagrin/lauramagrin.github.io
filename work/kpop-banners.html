Fan banners are a K-pop tradition: the crowd raises them at a planned moment of the show as a message to the artists. These two were voluntary projects for the fan community, each approved by Dreamcatcher Company before being printed and handed out: 4,000 banners in total. At the end of the shows, the artists picked up the banners and took photos with them.

UAU, the Dreamcatcher subunit formed by JiU, SuA and Yoohyeon, played São Paulo on August 15, 2025, on the "Playlist #You Are You" tour. The front carries a Korean message around "Good Luck", with the fandom's lightsticks and the group's mascots; the back shows the three members and the instructions for the crowd.

ChRocktikal's first world tour, "CRTK: The Beginning", reached São Paulo on June 21, 2026, produced by Highway Star. The front shows the band with the line "CRTK you've awakened us"; the back is a vintage radio with the instructions. Highway Star invited our fanbase to interview the band, and we published it with Dreamcatcher Company's authorization on YouTube, X and Instagram (@crtkupdates). I conducted the interview in Portuguese, English and Korean, with an on-site interpreter for the Portuguese–Korean exchanges; recording and editing are by Luisa Krummenauer, who manages @crtkupdates with me. The video passed 1,000 views on YouTube. For the same tour I also hand-drew four gift bags, one per member, to deliver the fans' gifts to the band.
